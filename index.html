★ Centenary Milestone · 1925 – 2025/26

A Century of Service to the Nation

1925 → 2026

Founded in 1925 in Calcutta under the visionary leadership of G.D. Birla, the Indian Chamber of Commerce (ICC) has stood as the vanguard of Indian enterprise through pre-independence industrialization, post-independence nation building, and the digital economy. Today, entering its second century, ICC establishes its state-of-the-art Madhya Pradesh Chapter.

“Industry and nation are inseparable partners in progress.”
Indian Chamber of Commerce · 100 Years of Excellence

Event Architecture

Transformative Ideas, Rapid-Fire

A dynamic, high-impact relay format designed to translate visionary thought leadership into Madhya Pradesh's actionable state policy agenda.

01

Sector Leaders Take the Stage

Chairs representing manufacturing, infra, agro-tech, FMCG, IT, and investments open with a focused read on current bottlenecks.

02

Rapid-Fire Solutions Relay

Each vertical presents actionable, high-yield policy interventions and collaborative industry frameworks to leapfrog state economic targets.

03

The Chapter's Working Agenda

Presentations synthesize into formal policy whitepapers submitted to the Government of Madhya Pradesh.
